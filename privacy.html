5.0
        

Statutory Rights & California Standards

        

Pursuant to the CCPA and high-standard transparency regulations, SOLVRAXIS maintains a "No-Sale" policy. We do not sell personal data to third-party data brokers or market aggregators.

        

Users retain the Right to Access, Right to Rectification, and Right to Erasure (The "Right to be Forgotten"). To exercise these rights, or to opt-out of persistent communications, users may utilize the account dashboard "Unsubscribe" mechanism.

      
      
        

Professional Commitment & Mechanical Necessity

        

SOLVRAXIS is built on the foundation of advocating for user success. Data sharing is limited to authorized licensees only when it constitutes a mechanical necessity to fulfill explicitly requested services.
